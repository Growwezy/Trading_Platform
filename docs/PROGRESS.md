- 2026-11-01: Two real bugs caught and fixed during live testing (not just code review): (1) `computeAdjustedPrice` was re-querying Postgres on every single tick for every open position and every WebSocket subscriber — fine at rest, but exhausted Prisma's connection pool within seconds under Binance's fast BTCUSDT trade stream, and even stalled unrelated requests sharing the same pool. Fixed with small in-memory caches (symbols 60s TTL, group markups 10s TTL) plus a 300ms per-symbol throttle in the tick processor. (2) The WebSocket connection handler did async tenant/auth lookups *before* attaching the `message` listener, racing a client's first message (sent immediately on `open`) against listener attachment — `ws` doesn't buffer events for late listeners, so subscribe requests were silently dropped. Fixed by moving that async resolution into the `upgrade` handler, before the socket is even accepted.
- 2026-11-01: Also caught live: the EURUSD seed spread (`baseSpreadPoints: 1`) was a full $1 price-unit wide instead of a realistic pip-sized spread, making a round-trip trade lose almost the entire notional. Fixed in `backend/prisma/seed.ts` (0.0002, a realistic ~2-pip spread) and made the seed's symbol `upsert` actually update existing rows on re-run (it previously only wrote on first create).
